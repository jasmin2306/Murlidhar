WHO WE ARE

Built on Trust. Driven by Excellence.

Since 2005, Murlidhar Mobile Stores has been building a legacy of Trust, Service & Excellence in the mobile industry. What began with a vision to offer the latest technology at the right price has grown into a trusted retail network of 9 branches across Ahmedabad.

With more than 20 years of industry experience, we have continuously evolved with technology while remaining committed to genuine products, competitive pricing and exceptional customer service.
